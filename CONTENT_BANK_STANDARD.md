# PET Quest V42 — Exam Content Bank Standard

Every exact mock pack must pass `validate_exam_pack.py`. It enforces the current structural contract used by PET Quest: Reading 32 items across 6 parts and Listening 25 items across 4 parts, including Reading Part 6 open cloze and Listening Part 3 gap fill.

Content quality still requires human academic review. Structural validation alone does not certify item difficulty, distractor quality, fairness or Cambridge equivalence.
