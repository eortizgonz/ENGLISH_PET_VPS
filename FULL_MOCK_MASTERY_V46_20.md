# PET Quest V46.20 — 20 Full Mock Mastery Bank

## Scope implemented

PET Quest now contains 20 complete mock forms, identified A through T.

Per form:
- Reading: 32 questions in 6 parts (5 + 5 + 5 + 5 + 6 + 6)
- Listening: 25 questions in 4 parts (7 + 6 + 6 + 6)
- Writing: 2 tasks (Part 1 email; Part 2 article/story choice)
- Speaking: 4 parts with explicit prompts/metadata

Portfolio totals:
- 640 Reading questions
- 500 Listening questions
- 40 Writing tasks
- 80 Speaking parts
- 1,140 scored Reading/Listening item IDs

## Listening independence

The 20 mocks use 300 distinct PET Audio Bank recordings:
- 140 Part 1 recordings
- 120 Part 2 recordings
- 20 Part 3 monologues
- 20 Part 4 interviews

Each mock therefore has 15 distinct recordings covering its 25 Listening questions. Part 3 correctly reuses one monologue for six responses; Part 4 correctly reuses one interview for six multiple-choice questions.

The former duplicated Listening content across Mock A/B/C has been replaced with distinct PET Audio Bank assignments.

## Writing and Speaking integration

Each mock card exposes Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking. Writing has a 45-minute paper screen, user-scoped local save and two task responses. Speaking exposes four pack-specific prompts and can route a selected part into the existing Speaking AI Examiner.

## Academic status

These are original PET Quest practice forms with machine-verified structural completeness. They are not claimed to be official Cambridge papers. External academic sign-off, pilot data, item statistics and psychometric form-equivalence remain required before claiming that all 20 forms are equivalent in difficulty.
