# PET Quest V46.17 — Anonymous Cambridge Scale Calibration

The calibration cohort stores no direct student identity. Required analytical fields: anonymous ID, PET Quest Readiness, external Cambridge English Scale score, source, exam date/cohort.

Current Cambridge B1 Preliminary result bands used by the lab:
- 160–170: Grade A, CEFR B2 (historically called Distinction)
- 153–159: Grade B, CEFR B1 (historically Merit)
- 140–152: Grade C, CEFR B1 (Pass)
- 120–139: Level A2
- below 120: below the B1/A2 certificate range used for cohort balance

Evidence maturity targets:
- 300+: minimum calibration cohort
- 500+: robust cohort target
- 1,000+: mature target

Scale equivalence stays OFF until cohort size, band balance, independent holdout accuracy and independent psychometric review gates pass.
