# PET Quest V46.23 — Child Learning Experience 100

Functional target: make the 9–14 experience complete as an educational product without weakening PET fidelity.

## Closed learning journey
1. APRENDER — explanations, examples and prominent “No entiendo”.
2. PRACTICAR — adaptive practice and immediate correction.
3. DOMINAR — 10-item remediation for the weakest diagnostic pattern.
4. SIMULAR — Full Mocks only after practice/mastery evidence.

## Child safeguards and motivation
- Short age-adapted daily goals.
- Mood check-in; “Necesito ayuda” opens support instead of penalising the learner.
- Optional break suggestion after sustained work; progress remains saved.
- Retry language after errors; errors are reframed as evidence for the next exercise.
- Mastery rewards require >=80% with >=5 attempts; a one-off lucky answer does not earn mastery.
- No social ranking or peer competition.
- Per-user local state plus server learning-events when authenticated.

## Age adaptation
- 9 years: 3-item micro-goal, short explanations.
- 10–12 years: 5-item goal, balanced guidance.
- 13–14 years: 8-item goal, more autonomy.

## Evidence boundary
“100/100” here means internal functional coverage of the specified child-learning UX contract. It is not a claim of external child-UX validation. Real usability validation with children, guardians/schools and independent academic review remains external evidence.
