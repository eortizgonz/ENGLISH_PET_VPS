# PET Quest V41 — Studio Audio Acceptance Requirements

The bundled MP3s are **reference prerecorded audio** for functional QA, not the final premium Listening bank. A commercial 10/10 Listening release must replace them with original, licensed studio recordings.

Required acceptance profile:
- natural British English as the primary exam-prep voice set;
- male and female speakers; adolescent and adult voices where appropriate;
- natural connected speech, stress, rhythm, intonation, reductions and distractors;
- multi-speaker dialogues/interviews where the task requires them;
- moderate contextual ambience only where it does not impair intelligibility;
- consistent loudness and clean mastering;
- each item reviewed against its answer key and transcript by two reviewers;
- rights/releases documented for every voice;
- `assets/audio/fidelity/audio_manifest.json` set to `production_ready: true` only after sign-off.

The production release gate treats studio audio as a blocker. Official Cambridge audio/material must not be bundled unless separately licensed.
